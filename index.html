Clinical interpretation requires context. PPV is most informative under controlled positive-pressure ventilation with regular cardiac rhythm and appropriate ventilatory conditions. It may be unreliable with spontaneous breaths, arrhythmia, low tidal volumes, altered thoracic compliance, or right ventricular dysfunction.

Do not use any single PPV cut-off as an automatic fluid administration instruction.

Method and limitations

PP = SBP − DBP for each individual heartbeat. PPV (%) = 100 × (PPmax − PPmin) / ((PPmax + PPmin) / 2).

For paired-pressure entry, each systolic and diastolic pressure must come from the same beat, selected by maximum or minimum PP—not independently selected maximum or minimum SBP/DBP. Values must be finite, with positive pulse pressures and PPmax ≥ PPmin. This application does not infer stroke-volume variation or clinical fluid responsiveness.

Calculations execute in your browser; measurements are not transmitted, stored, or sent to external APIs. The CSV export is generated locally. This is a research and educational measurement aid, not a medical device.
